## Working question

What changes when trustworthy AI is treated as a stress-testing discipline rather than a single metric?

## Draft outline

- Define the deployment shift before defining the score.
- Ask what private data is protected and what signals can still leak.
- Test both stability and plasticity when tasks evolve.
- Prefer evaluation setups that expose trade-offs instead of hiding them.
